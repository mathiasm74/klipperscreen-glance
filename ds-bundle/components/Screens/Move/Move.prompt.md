The move screen. Direct manipulation beats jog buttons: a 300mm bed map you tap or drag (snap dots on grid crossings, white live head dot, cyan target ring with a delayed "X 243 · Y 229" label offset from the finger) and a vertical Z height map with snap lines at 0/10/50/75/100mm (white live line, cyan target). Below the map: X / Y readout cells and a tappable speed cell cycling 50/150/300 mm/s. Right column: one-tap verbs (Home XYZ, QGL, Bed mesh, Z-offset, Motors off — gate verbs that need homing). Full-width "Precise moves" bar opens a stepper sheet for 0.01-10mm moves.
