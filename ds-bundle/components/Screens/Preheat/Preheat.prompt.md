The preheat screen. One horizontal temperature slider per heater spanning its useful range (100-270 nozzle with the left edge as off, 0-130 bed): amber fill to the live temp, white live tick, cyan target handle dragged with 5-degree snap; release commits, the left edge is off. Only the extruder's live temp gets Anton (58px). A sensor-only chamber renders as a narrow 10px status strip (faint fill, gray tick, no handle) - an instrument, not a control; a chamber heater would get a full slider. Below, a Mainsail-style 6-minute graph: heater PWM duty as translucent area fills rising from the plot floor, light fills under the temperature lines, dashed target lines with right-edge labels, wall-clock gridlines at even 2-minute marks. Right column (296px): material profiles (name + "nozzle / bed" pair) that set both targets in one tap - the active one takes the cyan selection fill; then Cool down (all targets to 0) and a green-text "Hold 150 / N" applying the keep-warm standby (nozzle 150, bed at profile temp). Amber phase throughout; the rail fills with heating progress and rides solid while holding.
