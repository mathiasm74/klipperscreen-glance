The extrude screen. Cyan prep phase. Hero: the live nozzle temp as the 160px Anton figure (amber while actively heating), a state subline, and a Preheat-grammar temperature slider spanning 100-270 (the dead 0-100 range is cut for drag resolution; the left edge is the off gesture). Everything that moves filament is gated on live temp >= min_extrude_temp - below it the verbs disarm and the subline says why. Right column (45%): big Retract / Extrude cards echoing the selected distance (Extrude is the green go-verb), Distance chips (5/10/15/25 mm) and Speed chips (1/2/5/10 mm/s) with the cyan selection, and Unload / Load macro buttons. Strokes queue: each tap fires its gcode immediately (the firmware serializes motion) and the bottom rail animates through each stroke's real duration with a bold dark "N x" count badge riding the fill's left end - always shown, even at 1x, so the queue teaches itself.
